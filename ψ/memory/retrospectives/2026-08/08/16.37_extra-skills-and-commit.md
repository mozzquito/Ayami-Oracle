## Lessons Learned

- **When copying a "skill" or similarly-packaged directory by name, always enumerate its full file tree first (`find $dir -type f`) rather than assuming a single entry-point file (like `SKILL.md`) is the whole package** — supporting scripts, templates, or assets living in subdirectories are easy to silently drop with a naive single-file copy, and the failure is invisible until someone tries to invoke the now-broken skill.
- **A pattern observed once earlier and generalized without re-verification is a trap** — "the last 4 skills I copied were single-file" is a sample of 4, not a proven invariant; verify the shape of each new batch rather than carrying forward an assumption from a small prior sample.
