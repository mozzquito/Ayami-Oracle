## Lessons Learned

- **When a user names a specific tool/file/process you don't recognize, search before declaring it doesn't exist.** "I don't have X" and "X doesn't exist on this machine" are different claims — only the first is something you can know from your own tool list alone. Cheap verification (grep/find) should come before a correction, not after pushback.
- **A locally-installed AI app with an "unlimited" model badge is very likely gated behind free-tier signup** — treat "unlimited free" marketing copy in third-party app UI as a claim to verify (via terms/signup flow), not a fact to relay uncritically, especially before suggesting a user invest time generating with it.
- **`/rrr`'s dig-miner session detection (`ls -t *.jsonl | head -1`) assumes one active session per project directory.** It breaks silently — no error, just wrong data — when a background/forked job runs concurrently with its parent, since "most recently modified" can resolve to the *other* session. Any skill that greps `~/.claude/projects/<encoded-pwd>/*.jsonl` by mtime needs a way to disambiguate concurrent sessions (e.g. matching on the job's own session/transcript id) rather than trusting recency alone.
