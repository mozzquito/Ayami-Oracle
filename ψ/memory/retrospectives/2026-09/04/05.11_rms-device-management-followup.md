## Lessons Learned

- When a follow-up session opens with an ambiguous noun that pattern-matches onto the immediately-preceding session's context (e.g. "device" right after a session about routers/VPN/DHCP), resist picking the most recent referent automatically — ask, especially when the different candidate meanings map to genuinely different UI actions with different consequences (unregistering a cloud account vs. deleting a VPN peer vs. deleting a DHCP reservation).
- When explaining a technical inference that connects two separate pieces of user-supplied evidence (e.g. "this private IP explains why that other thing failed"), say explicitly that it's an inference from the evidence pattern, not a confirmed fact — otherwise a plausible connection can get carried forward as settled truth in later sessions.
- For SaaS/cloud-vendor billing or pricing questions, explain the *mechanism* (how the system works) confidently, but explicitly disclaim live numbers (balances, exact rates) that require account access you don't have — point the user to the specific in-product page rather than guessing figures.
