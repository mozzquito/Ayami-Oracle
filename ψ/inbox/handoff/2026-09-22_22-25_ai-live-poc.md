3. **Design consult** (zcode ×2 + agy, in parallel, per CLAUDE.md's
   "consult where it pays off" rule): all three independently agreed
   `lipku/LiveTalking` **cannot run in real time on this 8GB M1 Mac** — its
   render backends (MuseTalk/wav2lip/ER-NeRF) need CUDA + 12GB+ VRAM. Gaps
   flagged: idle loop, turn-taking/queue, outbound safety filter (not just
   inbound), chunked/streaming TTS to avoid dead air, audio mixing.
   Recommended v1: skip real-time neural rendering, build an audio-only /
   2D-sprite avatar that actually runs locally; LiveTalking-on-rented-GPU is
   v3.
